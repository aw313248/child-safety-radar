# CareCub App Icon — Prompt 提案
> 給 Oscar 貼到 Midjourney / Google AI Studio (Imagen 3) 使用
> 更新：2026-05-01

---

## 使用說明

- **Midjourney**：貼進 Discord `/imagine prompt:` 或 web.midjourney.com
- **Google AI Studio (Imagen 3)**：在 Generate Images 欄位直接貼入
- 兩平台語法不同，請用對應版本
- 建議每個 prompt 生成 4 張後挑最好的截圖，回傳設計線評估

## Midjourney 用（貼 Discord 或 web）

### A1. 熊熊正面笑臉 — 標準版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered teddy bear character, chubby round face filling 80% of frame, enormous glossy eyes, wide open cheerful smile showing tiny teeth, soft brown fur with warm honey-gold highlights, wearing a tiny navy blue superhero cape peeking at the bottom, facing directly forward, centered composition, honey gold solid background #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown outline strokes #2B1810, warm soft studio lighting with subtle rim light, app icon design, no text, square 1:1, hyper-detailed fur texture, Pixar-quality 3D render --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 400
```

**設計思路**：最直接的品牌臉孔，熊熊佔滿畫面中心，蜂蜜金純色背景讓 icon 在 iOS 任何桌布上都醒目。類似 Duolingo 貓頭鷹的「吉祥物即品牌」策略。縮小到 60px 後熊臉輪廓依然清晰，辨識度最高。**推薦理由**：最符合家長「親近友善」直覺，適合主力方案。

### A2. 熊熊微側頭笑臉 — 活潑版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered chubby teddy bear character, head tilted slightly to the right at 15 degrees with playful energy, massive sparkling eyes with highlight dots, huge warm smile with rosy cheeks, soft honey-brown plush fur, tiny navy superhero collar visible at bottom edge, slight wink or squint giving mischievous charm, centered on honey gold background #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown ink outline #2B1810, warm diffused lighting, clean app icon framing, no text, square 1:1, studio-quality 3D character render --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 350
```

**設計思路**：微側頭的角度讓吉祥物從「靜態臉孔」變成有個性的角色，更像在跟用戶說「嘿！」腮紅增加親切感。適合強調 app 的活潑互動性。**推薦理由**：比 A1 多了個性，同樣在小尺寸清晰，作為 A1 的備選方案。

### A3. 熊熊超萌大眼特寫 — 兒童友善版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered baby bear face extreme close-up, enormous glassy doe eyes taking up one-third of the face, tiny cute button nose, big innocent open smile, extra chubby round cheeks with pink blush, super soft fluffy honey-brown fur with warm golden rim lighting, head fills entire frame leaving only thin background border, solid honey gold background #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown outline #2B1810, dreamy soft bokeh rim light, heartwarming expression, app icon square composition, no text, 1:1, Disney Pixar quality 3D render --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 500
```

**設計思路**：把眼睛比例放大，走嬰兒臉效應（baby face effect）— 觸發人類本能的保護欲與親切感。孩子看到不會害怕，家長看到覺得安心。**推薦理由**：對 0–6 歲目標族群的家長最有共鳴，情緒衝擊力最強。

### A4. 熊熊俏皮歪頭笑 — 品牌個性版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered teddy bear character, head tilted 25 degrees left, one eye slightly squinting with playful expression, other eye wide open, big goofy grin showing happiness, soft warm plush brown fur, honey-toned highlights in fur, subtle CC golden badge hint on chest, centered on rich honey gold circle vignette background fading to #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown outlines and shadows #2B1810, warm three-point lighting, app icon design, no text, square 1:1, premium 3D character animation quality --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 300
```

**設計思路**：大角度歪頭配不對稱眼神，創造獨特記憶點。品牌個性最強，用戶在 app store 一排 icon 中最容易記住這個。胸前隱約的 CC 徽章暗示品牌縮寫。**推薦理由**：若想讓 CareCub 有鮮明辨識度，選這個方向。

### B1. 熊熊舉盾守護 — 信任感版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered teddy bear character holding a small rounded shield in front, shield has a warm golden heart symbol on it, bear has a brave determined but still adorable expression, big bright eyes full of confidence, soft brown plush fur, tiny navy superhero cape flowing behind, standing proud centered in frame, honey gold solid background #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown outline #2B1810, shield catches warm directional light creating depth, app icon square design, no text, 1:1, Pixar-quality 3D render --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 400
```

**設計思路**：盾牌是普世「守護/安全」的視覺語言，家長一眼看懂 app 功能。熊熊保持可愛表情而非威嚴，平衡信任感與親切感。**推薦理由**：最能傳達「兒童守護」產品定位，適合 App Store 截圖旁邊的 icon。

### B2. 熊熊抱愛心 — 溫暖版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered chubby teddy bear character hugging a large glowing golden heart to its chest, eyes closed in contentment with a soft warm smile, fur glowing with honey-warm rim light, the heart emits a soft golden radiance, bear is centered, honey gold background #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown outline strokes #2B1810, warm ambient glow lighting, heartwarming and trustworthy mood, app icon design, no text, square 1:1, premium animated movie quality 3D render --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 450
```

**設計思路**：愛心代表「愛護孩子」，熊熊閉眼微笑傳遞安心感而非警覺感。整體情緒偏柔和溫暖，適合強調「家長安心」這個情感主軸。**推薦理由**：情感衝擊力強，可搭配「讓你安心的每一天」文案方向。

### B3. 熊熊戴守護勳章 — 可信度版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered teddy bear character wearing a round golden badge/medal on its chest with a tiny shield symbol, standing proudly with arms slightly out, confident happy expression with bright eyes and wide smile, soft warm plush brown fur, honey gold and navy blue color scheme, centered on honey gold background #F2B84B, dark cocoa brown outline #2B1810, directional warm studio light with medal catching highlight, app icon square composition, no text, 1:1, high-end 3D character illustration quality --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 380
```

**設計思路**：勳章/徽章暗示「認證、可信賴」的形象，比盾牌更品牌化，更容易和 CareCub 的 CC 標誌結合。熊熊自信站姿不失可愛。**推薦理由**：若日後要做品牌延伸（認證標章、合作標誌），此版本視覺語言最一致。

### B4. 熊熊守護光環 — 科技感版

```
A super cute kawaii 3D rendered teddy bear character surrounded by a soft glowing circular halo ring in warm honey gold, subtle sparkle particles floating around, bear has a gentle protective expression with kind eyes and soft smile, one paw slightly raised as if casting a protective spell, warm brown plush fur, centered composition with halo framing the figure, honey gold background #F2B84B with subtle radial gradient, dark cocoa brown outlines #2B1810, magical soft glow lighting, app icon design, no text, square 1:1, premium 3D render --ar 1:1 --v 6.1 --q 2 --stylize 420
```

**設計思路**：光環暗示「AI 掃描/科技守護」的概念，同時保持卡通可愛感。閃爍粒子增加動感，讓靜態 icon 有活力。兼顧「AI 產品」與「可愛品牌」兩個訴求。**推薦理由**：最能傳達「AI 幫你守護」的科技感，適合強調技術優勢的行銷方向。

## Google AI Studio (Imagen 3) 用

> 直接貼入 Generate Images 文字框，不需要 `--` 指令

---

### A1-NB. 熊熊正面笑臉 — 標準版

```
Create a high-quality 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character for a mobile app icon. The bear has a chubby round face centered in the frame, occupying about 80% of the image. It features enormous shiny eyes, a wide happy smile, and soft warm brown fur with golden highlights. The bear is wearing a tiny navy blue superhero cape visible at the bottom. Place the character on a solid honey gold background (hex #F2B84B). Use dark cocoa brown outlines (hex #2B1810) to define the shapes. The lighting should be warm and soft like a studio portrait. Style: Pixar-quality 3D character render, kawaii, child-friendly, app icon composition, no text, perfectly square 1:1 format.
```

**設計思路**：Imagen 3 對自然語言描述反應好，細節越具體越好。這版用「app icon composition」引導輸出比例與構圖，「Pixar-quality 3D」確保品質感。對應 Midjourney A1。

---

### A2-NB. 熊熊微側頭笑臉 — 活潑版

```
Generate a premium 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character suitable for a mobile app icon. The bear's head is tilted slightly to the right at about 15 degrees, giving a playful and lively feeling. It has large sparkling eyes with bright highlight dots, a warm big smile, and rosy pink cheeks. The fur is soft honey-brown with warm lighting. A small navy blue superhero collar peeks at the bottom edge of the frame. Background: solid honey gold color (#F2B84B). Outlines: dark cocoa brown (#2B1810). Warm, diffused studio lighting. Style: high-end 3D animation quality, kawaii aesthetic, no text, 1:1 square format.
```

**設計思路**：Imagen 3 對角度描述需要用「about X degrees」這樣的語言更精確。腮紅用「rosy pink cheeks」確保生成。對應 Midjourney A2。

---

### A3-NB. 熊熊超萌大眼特寫 — 兒童友善版

```
Create an adorable kawaii 3D rendered baby bear face close-up for a mobile app icon. The bear has oversized, glossy doe eyes that take up nearly a third of the face, conveying innocence and warmth. The nose is tiny and button-like. The expression is a soft, open-mouth smile radiating happiness. The cheeks are extra chubby with gentle pink blush. The honey-brown fur is ultra-soft and fluffy, lit with warm golden rim lighting. The bear's head fills almost the entire frame with just a thin border of honey gold background (#F2B84B). Dark cocoa brown outlines (#2B1810). Style: Disney/Pixar 3D quality, dream-like soft focus, emotionally warm, child-safe aesthetic, no text, square 1:1.
```

**設計思路**：「baby face effect」相關描述用「oversized eyes」「button-like nose」「chubby cheeks」拆解，讓 Imagen 3 理解造型比例意圖。對應 Midjourney A3。

---

### A4-NB. 熊熊俏皮歪頭笑 — 品牌個性版

```
Generate a highly expressive 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character for a mobile app icon. The bear's head is tilted about 25 degrees to the left with a goofy, joyful expression. One eye is slightly squinting playfully while the other is wide open with excitement. The mouth shows a big enthusiastic grin. The warm honey-brown plush fur has golden highlights. A small circular gold badge hint is visible on the chest. The background is a rich honey gold (#F2B84B) with a subtle circular vignette. Outlines in dark cocoa brown (#2B1810). Style: premium 3D character animation, kawaii, distinct personality, no text, square 1:1 format.
```

**設計思路**：Imagen 3 對「goofy, joyful expression」和不對稱眼神這種描述需要拆開說「one eye squinting / other eye wide」才夠準確。對應 Midjourney A4。

### B1-NB. 熊熊舉盾守護 — 信任感版

```
Create a 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character holding a small rounded shield in front of its body, designed as a mobile app icon. The shield is golden with a heart symbol on it. The bear has a brave yet adorable expression — determined eyes and a confident smile. It wears a tiny navy blue superhero cape flowing behind. The fur is soft and warm honey-brown. Center the bear in the frame against a solid honey gold background (#F2B84B). Use dark cocoa brown outlines (#2B1810). The shield catches warm directional light creating dimension. Style: Pixar 3D quality, trustworthy and child-friendly mood, app icon format, no text, 1:1 square.
```

**設計思路**：盾牌加愛心讓守護概念視覺化，Imagen 3 對「catches warm directional light」能生成有立體感的物件光影。對應 Midjourney B1。

### B2-NB. 熊熊抱愛心 — 溫暖版

```
Generate a 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character hugging a large glowing golden heart close to its chest, for a mobile app icon. The bear's eyes are softly closed in contentment, with a gentle warm smile. The heart emits a soft golden glow around the bear. The fur is fluffy and warm honey-brown. Center the character on a honey gold background (#F2B84B). Dark cocoa brown outlines (#2B1810). Warm ambient glow lighting making the whole scene feel safe and nurturing. Style: premium animated movie quality 3D render, heartwarming, emotional warmth, no text, perfectly square 1:1 format.
```

**設計思路**：「eyes closed in contentment」比「happy expression」更能描述這種柔和安心的情緒，Imagen 3 對情緒類描述較敏感。對應 Midjourney B2。

### B3-NB. 熊熊戴守護勳章 — 可信度版

```
Create a 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character standing confidently with a round golden badge/medal on its chest featuring a small shield symbol, designed for a mobile app icon. The bear has a proud, happy expression with bright eyes and a wide smile. Its arms are slightly extended in a welcoming stance. The fur is soft warm plush honey-brown. Background: solid honey gold (#F2B84B). Outlines: dark cocoa brown (#2B1810). Warm directional studio light with the medal catching a highlight. Style: high-end 3D character illustration, trustworthy and friendly, kawaii aesthetic, no text, 1:1 square format.
```

**設計思路**：「welcoming stance」讓熊熊站姿更自然，「medal catching a highlight」引導生成有光澤的金屬質感勳章。對應 Midjourney B3。

---

### B4-NB. 熊熊守護光環 — 科技感版

```
Generate a 3D rendered kawaii teddy bear character surrounded by a soft glowing circular halo ring in warm golden honey color, for a mobile app icon. Small sparkle particles float gently around the bear. The bear has a gentle, protective expression — kind eyes and a soft reassuring smile — with one paw slightly raised as if casting a protective spell. The fur is warm honey-brown. Background: honey gold (#F2B84B) with a subtle radial gradient glow emanating from behind the bear. Dark cocoa brown outlines (#2B1810). Magical, warm, soft lighting. Style: premium animated 3D render, technological warmth, kawaii, child-safe, no text, square 1:1 format.
```

**設計思路**：「casting a protective spell」給熊熊動作提供動機，讓姿勢更自然。「technological warmth」引導 Imagen 3 走科技感但不冷漠的方向。對應 Midjourney B4。

## 生成建議

| 工具 | 建議設定 |
|---|---|
| Midjourney | Vary (Subtle) 3–4 次選最好的；若太卡通加 `--style raw` |
| Imagen 3 | 每個 prompt 點 Generate 4 張；若顏色不夠準確，在 prompt 加「exact hex color #F2B84B for background」 |
